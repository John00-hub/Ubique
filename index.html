The Firm

A considered approach to legal work.

Founded in 2020, Ubique Legal is a full-service law firm headquartered in Ibadan, with correspondence offices in Lagos and Ogun State. We advise and represent individuals, families, entrepreneurs, companies and institutions across a broad range of legal matters.

Our role is to make the law useful: to understand what is at stake, identify the real options and give clients a confident path forward. From a contract that needs a second look to a dispute that needs determined advocacy, we bring diligence, discretion and practical judgment to every brief.

“Clear advice is not a luxury. It is the beginning of a better decision.”The Ubique Legal standard

AO

Akinkunmi Abayomi Odumosu, Esq., ACIArb(UK)

Principal PartnerACIArb

Akinkunmi received his LL.B. from Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, Osun State, and was called to the Nigerian Bar in 2016. He received his LL.M. from the University of Ibadan, Oyo State, in 2019. He worked extensively in the office of a renowned Senior Advocate of Nigeria, where he rose to Head of Chambers before co-founding the firm. He has been in active litigation practice since his call to the Bar, conducting cases at lower and appellate courts.

  • Election Petitions — part of the team behind the successful gubernatorial election petitions of the current Governors of Oyo and Osun States.
  • Real Estate — extensive practice across land and property matters.
  • Matrimonial Cases — on the team that handled and concluded a particularly difficult divorce involving a high-ranking military officer, which went on interlocutory appeal and back to the trial court.
  • Alternative Dispute Resolution — co-led a team that settled a series of fiercely contested cases in Lagos and Ibadan involving properties worth over ₦4 billion, out of court.

He is an active member of the Nigerian Bar Association, Ibadan branch, and served on its Welfare Committee (2022–2024). For leisure he enjoys football and reading. He is happily married with two children.

KN

Kelechi Opeoluwa Nnamadim, Esq.

Managing Partner and Notary PublicNotary Public

Kelechi received her LL.B. from the University of Lagos and was called to the Nigerian Bar in 2016. She received her LL.M. from the University of Ibadan, Oyo State, in 2021. Before co-founding the firm, she worked at — and eventually became Head of Chambers of — a reputable law firm in Ibadan. She was appointed Notary Public for Nigeria by the Chief Justice of Nigeria in 2026, and has conducted cases at state, federal and appellate courts.

  • Corporate & Commercial Law — worked for two tier-2 commercial banks for almost six years, handling their diverse litigation from drafting to judgment (debt recovery, recovery of assets, land disputes, garnishee proceedings); external Solicitor to the Asset Management Corporation of Nigeria (AMCON), co-leading teams that recovered debts worth billions of naira in Lagos and Ibadan. She also handles company registrations and regulatory compliance.
  • Civil Litigation — has solely conducted several civil disputes, including breach of contract and land disputes, single-handedly conducting the trials; in 2023, co-led the team that concluded a protracted land dispute filed in 1999 by out-of-court settlement, worth about ₦200 million.
  • Family Law — has handled several matrimonial and child-custody matters of varying complexity.
  • Intellectual Property — handles the registration of intellectual and industrial property, as well as disputes arising from them.

She is an active member of the Nigerian Bar Association, Ibadan branch, the NBA Women's Forum, and FIDA (International Federation of Women Lawyers), Oyo State. For leisure she enjoys music, baking and reading. She is happily married with two children.

SO

Chief Sunday Afolorunsho Odumosu

ConsultantNotary Public

Chief Sunday received his LL.B. from the then Ogun State University (now Olabisi Onabanjo University), Ago-Iwoye, Ogun State, and was called to the Nigerian Bar in 1988. He has been in active litigation practice for nearly four decades, conducting cases at state, federal and appellate courts. He was appointed Notary Public for Nigeria by the Chief Justice of Nigeria in 2019.

  • Criminal Litigation — has handled matters from simple offences to misdemeanours, felonies and capital offences.
  • Civil Litigation — has represented clients in diverse civil disputes, particularly chieftaincy matters, land disputes and breach of contract.

He is an active member of the Nigerian Bar Association, Ijebu-Ode branch, and served as its Chairman (2008–2010). For leisure he enjoys reading. He is happily married with three children.
